Alex is an SSW UI/UX Designer who specialises in product design, strategy and agentic engineering. He designs digital products for SSW clients in finance, enterprise operations, travel and education, and takes them from discovery and user research to coded prototypes and developer handover. Alex writes the front end as well as designing it, directing AI agents to build working software and keep design systems consistent, with WCAG accessibility built in from the first commit. He works alongside engineers and product owners from the first workshop to the final handover.

### Professional Development

Previously, Alex helped Australian startups shape their products toward product-market fit. Before that, he spent 5 years as a carpenter – so he doesn't shy away from a hard day's work and knows what it means to build with care and intention. Outside of work, you'll find him mastering his favourite video games, fixing his car (again), growing and propagating plants, firing up the barbecue with mates, or talking design over a beer or coffee.

### Selected Work at SSW

**Enterprise Pricing & Rebates Platform** An electronics distributor had to replace the decades-old system that ran its pricing, rebates and customer claims, with a fixed deadline and developers already building. To answer a simple question, staff searched across several systems, and every team (often every person) kept their own spreadsheets up to date by hand. The copies drifted apart, and people kept stalling on someone else's data or redoing work another team had already done. Working on site alongside two SSW developers, Alex fed what staff told him back into the build. The client saw the findings within days, and the developers got clear decisions plus a list of what to stop building. Alex proposed one source of truth with a worklist on top, so each person could see what was waiting on them. He built most of the front end and its design system himself, using agentic workflows to keep design and code in step. At handover the client had a working prototype, specs for 14 screens and a log of design decisions with the reasoning behind each.

**Loan Settlement Tracking Platform** Brokers and lenders were phoning in hundreds of times a month to ask where their loan was up to. Alex set out to show them where each loan stood, so they had no reason to call. He started by walking through the whole settlement process with the client's team and kept them involved from there: real users tested the prototype early, and the client reviewed progress at regular checkpoints. He built the front end on a shared design system and used an agentic engineering workflow for much of the build, closing 270+ tickets in about seven weeks. The client made the key calls with test results in hand, including the decision to launch the MVP without AI features. Alex's handover covered the business, process and UX decisions for the build team.

**AI Careers Pathway Chatbot** School leavers trying to get into a trade face a maze of state-by-state licensing rules, and generic AI chatbots get those details wrong. Before the sprint, Alex agreed a brief with the client that set out who owned which decisions, so their team was steering from day one. Over three days they shaped a mobile-first assistant where the AI handles the conversation and the licensing facts come from verified data. Each conversation ends with a saved career pathway and an introduction to a local training provider, the result the client reports on. Alex's coded prototype became the basis for SSW's spec and estimate. SSW developers then took it into a full app, and he kept building the UI alongside them, along with a walkthrough the client could present to their board and funders.

**White-Label Travel Platform** A corporate travel platform needed to launch a partner brand, but copy-pasted code had pulled its two portals apart. 15 components existed twice and had started to diverge, and form styling had split 14 ways, so a rebrand meant doing the work twice. Alex mapped the drift across four frontends and built an accessible brand palette, with a script that checks the contrast of each colour pair. He then spent a day on site with the client's engineers working out how to separate brand styling from platform styling, and finished with a live demo of a brand swap. Because the engineers had shaped the approach, their team shipped the shared foundation within three days.

### Earlier Work

**Energy Optimisation Platform** Alex joined mid-build, bringing structure and simplicity to a rapidly evolving product. Working closely with the CEO and engineers, he facilitated cross-functional workshops, made design decisions aligned with business goals, and used Lean UX methods to iterate quickly as the product matured. He inherited a component library originally built for another domain and, in collaboration with developers, laid the foundation for a cohesive design system by repurposing what already existed.

**Street Furniture Manufacturing** Alex led a complete brand and digital evolution for a street furniture manufacturer whose reputation for quality wasn't reflected in their brand or online presence – costing them project opportunities. As full-stack designer leveraging AI workflows, he delivered brand strategy, visual identity, user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity UI. He then built the site in Webflow CMS with dynamic search filters, geolocation integration, a 3D product configurator, and SEO.
